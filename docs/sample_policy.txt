Company Leave Policy

Employees are eligible for 15 days of paid vacation leave per year. Vacation requests must be submitted at least two weeks in advance and approved by the manager.

Sick Leave Policy

Employees are allowed 8 sick leave days per year. If sick leave exceeds 3 continuous days, medical documentation may be required.

Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Remote employees must be available during core business hours from 10 AM to 4 PM.

Expense Reimbursement Policy

Employees can submit reimbursement claims for approved business expenses such as travel, meals, and office supplies. Receipts are required for all claims.

Benefits Policy

Full-time employees are eligible for health insurance, dental insurance, vision insurance, 401k benefits, and wellness programs.
